| **Containers of a killed run no longer pile up in podman's storage.** When Coxswain was closed by force or crashed during a chess analysis, a preview or a conversion, podman kept that container in its storage, holding its image so it could not be removed. Coxswain now removes such leftovers of its own the next time it starts a container, and stops each container by name when its run ends. | [Containers](previews/containers.md#leftovers-of-a-run-that-was-killed) |
