| **A 7z split into parts opens like a folder, and large 7z archives open at all.** **Enter** on `games.7z.001` shows the whole archive, its `.002`, `.003` … parts read as one, read-only, in both apps; inside a zip too, its parts copied together into the cache. The later parts say in their facts which part they are and where the archive opens from. A 7z with up to about 900,000 files opens (before: about 250,000), and a file taken out of a 7z made of many blocks is unpacked from its own block: a game from a 7z of 400,000 Go games shows in 0.3 s instead of 5 s. In the terminal app, **F3** and **Alt+K** on a Go or chess file inside an archive open its games on the board, as on disk. Nothing is read again. | [Archives as folders](files/archives.md#limits) | <!-- feature -->
