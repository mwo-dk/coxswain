| **Transcripts of speech with Whisper.** Turn on *Transcribe speech with Whisper* under Settings → Finding files, then *Transcribe speech* in the action menu (Shift+F10) or F9 on a recording, a video or a folder: it says how many hours of sound and how long they take here, then transcribes in the background, never on battery, pausable from the *Speech* line. Find finds what was said; the desktop app's preview shows the transcript with its times, the terminal app's F3. The model is chosen for this machine and downloaded from Hugging Face (checked by its SHA-256) when you turn it on; an installed whisper.cpp runs first, else `ghcr.io/ggml-org/whisper.cpp:main` (about 505 MB) without network. Read again: nothing; only the files you ask for are transcribed, and again only when they change. | [Transcripts of speech](search/transcripts.md) |
