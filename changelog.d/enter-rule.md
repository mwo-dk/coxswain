| **Enter goes into anything that holds things and opens games on their board, in both apps.** **Enter** (or a double-click) opens a chess, Go, draughts, xiangqi, shogi or bridge file, a SCID or ChessBase database, EPD positions or an opening book in the games window, a file of one game straight on its board, on the disk and inside archives, nested ones too; a SCID or ChessBase database inside a zip now opens (its files come out together), and a `.cbv` inside a zip shows its games in the terminal app instead of hex. A disk image inside an archive opens like a folder while disk images open, and a zip split into parts (`.zip.001`) opens read-only like a 7z in parts. The command line (the status bar in the desktop app) says what **Enter** does on the entry under the cursor: *Enter: Open games*, *Enter: Open archive*, *Enter: Open container image*. **F3** shows the file itself, and games as before. | [What Enter and F3 do](commands/opening-files.md#what-enter-and-f3-do) | <!-- feature -->
