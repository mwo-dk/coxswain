# Lisbon, 12–16 May

Flight SK 1745 leaves Copenhagen at 07:10 and lands at 10:05. Hotel in Alfama, two nights, then an apartment near Príncipe Real. Day two: tram 28 early before the crowds, the castle, lunch at the market. Day three: train to Sintra, Pena palace tickets booked for 10:30. Day four: Belém, the tower and the custard tarts. Return flight on the sixteenth at 18:40.
