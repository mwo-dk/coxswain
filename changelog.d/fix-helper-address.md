| **Find searches inside files again when the search helper was slow to start, and no other program can pose as the helper.** An app that gave up waiting for the helper looks for it again every 30 seconds and uses it once it answers, instead of searching names only until it is restarted. An app dials the helper only while one runs, and only takes an answer that carries a second token from the helper's private address file, so another program on the port a killed helper left behind gets neither your searches nor a say in the results. The name index and the helper's address are written under names of their own, so an app and the helper saving at once no longer spoil `index.bin` or `index.addr`. | [The search helper](search/helper.md#how-it-works) |
