| **Choose which viewers Coxswain loads.** Settings → Modules, in both apps and offered in the first-run guide, turns off the viewers you do not want: documents, Office, e-books, LaTeX, HTML, diagrams, data, media, games and more, each with its formats and what it uses. A file whose module is off is shown as text or hex, and the first time the status line says which module would show it. All are on until you turn one off (`[modules] off`). | [Modules: choose the viewers](customise/modules.md) | <!-- feature -->
