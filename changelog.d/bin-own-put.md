| **F8 from a disk without a bin of its own moves file by file, and can be stopped.** On FreeBSD, the other BSDs and Linux, a file or folder from such a disk (or from a bind mount of your home's disk) now goes to the home bin as **F6** moves between disks: a link goes as a link instead of being refused or followed, **Esc** stops it, and a file that cannot be read or removed brings everything back instead of leaving a hidden part in the bin that the next Empty took.
