| **Xiangqi games from Chinese websites open on the board.** DhtmlXQ games, the format of the xiangqi sites and of community collections exported from them (one game a file, often with no ending, or `.che`), show in the table of games and on the xiangqi board with their variations and comments, in both apps, inside a zip too: rest the cursor on one with the preview pane open, or press **F3** in the terminal app. All but 13 of the 191,234 games in one such collection play to their end.
