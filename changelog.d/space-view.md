| **See where the space goes.** **Alt+U** (also in **F9**, the action menu, the preview pane next to a folder's *Total size*, and a drive's menu in the sidebar) walks a folder or a drive and shows it as a treemap: every file a rectangle by its size, inside its folder's, coloured by its kind (pictures, videos, music, archives, documents, code, data, programs, disk images). Beside it the bytes by type, with a click lighting up that type's rectangles, and the 100 largest files. Hover or the arrows show a file's path, size and type; Enter or a click puts the panel's cursor on it; a right-click or **+** zooms into a folder, **Backspace** out. **F8** deletes from there as in the panels, and the map follows. It stays on one disk: a disk mounted inside is a block of its own that opens Space there. Files only in the cloud count 0 bytes and are never read. Both apps; the terminal app draws it in coloured half-blocks. | [Space](panels/space.md) |
