| **A move to another disk never loses a file.** A move across disks that fails half way (a full disk, an unreadable file, a pipe in the folder) now moves back what had gone over, so the source is whole again; if that fails too, the error names both places and **Ctrl+Z** takes back the rest. A target that is the source itself under another path (a bind mount) is refused instead of being emptied, and in the terminal app an accented letter typed right after **Esc** no longer crashes it. | [Move and rename](files/move-and-rename.md#a-move-to-another-disk-failed-half-way-where-are-my-files) |
