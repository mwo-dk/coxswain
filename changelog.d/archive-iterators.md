| **Big logs and text files inside archives are searched like those on disk, and a 7z of 900,000 files opens.** Text, code, CSV and logs inside a zip, tar or 7z are read for search as they are unpacked, never copied to the disk first, whatever their size: their first 4 MB of text kept, all of it in a folder kept whole (*Long files* → *Everything*), with a passage for every part for search by meaning; a long one in a sampled folder gets its passages from all of it too. A zip of 200,000 files lists three times faster in half the memory, and one of its files previews in 0.04 s instead of 0.5 s. A 7z's list of contents is read as it is unpacked, so one naming 900,000 files opens as a folder and its files preview and copy out, where before it was refused. A zip, tar or 7z inside another archive is looked into by search too, three deep and up to 256 MB each: its files are found by name and text. A mailbox's message count no longer reads every message. Nothing is read again by itself: files inside archives already in the store keep what they have until their archive changes, or until a folder's *Long files* setting changes. | [Search inside archives](search/archives.md#limits) · [Archives as folders](files/archives.md#limits) | <!-- feature -->
