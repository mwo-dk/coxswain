| **ChessBase games come with their annotations, ChessBase archives open, and comments draw on the board.** A `.cbh` database's texts, symbols, evaluations and times show with the moves, and its coloured squares and arrows on the board, as do `[%csl]` and `[%cal]` in any PGN comment, in both apps. A `.cbv` archive opens as the database it holds. Chess960 games are read, a set-up position gets the castling rights its moves use, and databases past 4 GB find their games through `.cbj`. The search store reads each `.cbv` file once, for the first time; nothing else is read again (Chess960 games of a `.cbh` file it already has come in when that file changes). | [ChessBase databases](previews/chess.md#chessbase-databases) | <!-- feature -->
