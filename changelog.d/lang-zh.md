| **Coxswain speaks Chinese, twice: 中文（台灣） in Traditional characters with Taiwan's words, and 中文（新加坡） in Simplified characters.** Both apps, every text, with their flags in Settings → *Looks* → *Language*; *Automatic* picks one by the script your system names. The desktop app draws them with PingFang, Microsoft JhengHei or YaHei, or Noto Sans CJK, and on the BSDs and Linux says once which font to install when none is there; the terminal app lines them up at double width. Names sort by strokes in 中文（台灣） and by pinyin in 中文（新加坡）. Both are marked *new* for four weekly releases. | [Languages](customise/languages.md#the-languages) · [Chinese, Japanese and Korean](customise/languages.md#chinese-japanese-and-korean) | <!-- feature -->
