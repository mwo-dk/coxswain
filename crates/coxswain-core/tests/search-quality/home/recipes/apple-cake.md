# Grandma's apple cake

Peel and slice six sour apples and cook them with a little sugar and a vanilla pod until soft. Crush two hundred grams of rye bread crumbs with butter and brown sugar in a hot pan until crisp. In a glass bowl, make layers of apple purée and crumbs, and finish with whipped cream and a spoon of redcurrant jelly. Serve cold; it is best the day after.
