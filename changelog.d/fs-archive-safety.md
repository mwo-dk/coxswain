| **Safer copies, moves and archives.** A move (**F6**) of a folder into an archive is refused when the folder holds a symbolic link, a pipe or a device, which the archive would leave out and the move would then have deleted. Find duplicates scans a folder ticked inside another ticked folder once, so on Windows no file is shown as its own duplicate and the bin never takes the only copy. Copying a pipe no longer hangs (pipes, sockets and devices are refused), a new folder is copied under a passing name and named when complete, so a failed copy leaves nothing and can be tried again, and copied files keep their modification date. A 7z whose list of contents unpacks to more than 16 MB (was 64 MB) is not opened, as a crafted one could fill the memory. **Ctrl+E** leaves out a tar's symbolic links and devices, and on Windows names such as `NUL` and `CON` are not written from any archive. | [Copy](files/copy.md#what-you-see) |
