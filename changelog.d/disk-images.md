| **Disk images, CHM help files and CBR comics.** The preview pane shows what an `.iso`, `.dmg`, `.vhd`, `.vhdx`, `.vmdk`, `.qcow2`, `.vdi`, `.wim` or raw `.img` is: its format, the size of the disk it holds, what it takes on your disk, fixed or growing, its partitions (MBR or GPT) and its label, read from its headers with no program needed; the terminal app shows them in Properties (**Alt+Enter**). Turn on *Settings → Behaviour → Open disk images, CHM and CBR like folders* (`[archive] disk_images`, off by default; also in **Shift+F10** and **F9** on an image) and **Enter** opens an image, a `.chm` help file or a `.cbr` comic like a folder, read-only, with an installed 7-Zip: **F5** copies files out, nothing is ever mounted or written into it, and search does not read inside. Under the facts a line says what to do next: turn it on, the line that installs 7-Zip here, or **Enter**. A `.vhd` that is VHDL source stays code. Both apps. | [Disk images](files/disk-images.md) |
