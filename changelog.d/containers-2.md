| **Make PDF searchable, and previews of Typst, PostScript, XPS, D2 and ten more diagram kinds, old office files and Windows clip art. Read again once for search: `.ps` and `.eps` files, and where LibreOffice is installed its `.wri`, `.wk1`, `.wks`, `.123`, `.sxc`, `.abw`, `.cwk` and `.numbers` files.** **Alt+P** (also in Shift+F10, F9 and on a PDF's preview, which offers it on a scan) writes `name.searchable.pdf` next to a scanned PDF with its words in it, using OCRmyPDF, installed or in its container, in both apps; the original is never changed, the status line shows how far it is, **Stop** (or **Alt+P** again) ends it, and quitting stops it first. The desktop app now previews Typst (`.typ`), PostScript (`.ps`, `.eps`, with Ghostscript and `-dSAFER`), XPS (with an installed `gxps`), D2, ditaa, svgbob, Pikchr, nomnoml, WaveDrom, Vega, Vega-Lite, DBML, erd and bytefield, Windows clip art (`.wmf`, `.emf`) and old office files (Works, Windows Write, WordPerfect, Lotus 1-2-3, StarOffice, AbiWord, ClarisWorks, iWork). Each uses an installed program first, else its image (Typst's, Kroki's, OCRmyPDF's, and Gotenberg's for LibreOffice), with no network, pulled only after a click that says its size; Settings and Disk use say what each image gives and costs. PostScript is read again because it now has a reader of its own; the office kinds because LibreOffice now reads them.
