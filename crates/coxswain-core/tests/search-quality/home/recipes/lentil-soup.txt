Red lentil soup for four. Fry an onion, two cloves of garlic and a piece of ginger in oil, add a teaspoon of cumin and one of turmeric, then 250 g red lentils, a tin of chopped tomatoes and a litre of vegetable stock. Simmer for twenty minutes, blend half of it and finish with lemon juice and fresh coriander. Freezes well.
