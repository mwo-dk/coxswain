| **Large EPUB books and web pages are read with a fraction of the memory, and long ones are found by what their last chapters say.** A web page and each chapter of a book are read as they come, never whole: a 60 MB chapter or page takes 11 to 12 MB to read instead of 65 to 68 MB, and a page saved with a 30 MB picture inside it 8 MB instead of 34 MB. The text found is the same (checked on 3,000 real pages). A book or page with about 3 MB of text or more gets its passages for search by meaning from all of it, read for at most 20 seconds (the time the model takes not counted): only those `.epub`, `.html`, `.htm` and `.xhtml` files get their vectors made again, once; nothing is read again for search by words. The EPUB preview shows the first megabyte of a long chapter, and **F3** in the terminal app shows an `.epub` as its text. | [Documents it reads](search/documents.md#is-a-large-epub-or-web-page-read-whole) | <!-- feature -->
