| **Hostile archives and histories stay bounded, and the once-only redo of PGN positions is once only.** A 7z whose header claims billions of entries, or a name as long as the header, is refused before any memory is taken; a `.cbv` whose members together claim far more than their packed bytes can hold is refused as one member is; a diff line of megabytes in a commit's changes is cut at 1 MB, not held whole; a container image layer of endless whiteouts counts towards the files limit. Re-done on your machine: the once-only remaking of PGN positions (2.35) now goes on where it got to across scans instead of starting over, so a PGN file whose last game has no moves, or with more games than positions are kept for, has its positions made again once, not at every scan while another PGN file waits for its unplugged disk; a PGN file with more games than its text keeps is left be. Commit changes that could not be read (no `hg`, a failing `git`) are tried again at the next scan instead of being marked read. | [Chess](previews/chess.md#finding-games) · [Archives](files/archives.md) |
