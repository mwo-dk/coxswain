| **A tar whose listing could differ from what comes out of it is refused.** A tar with a sparse file, or a size given in a PAX record apart from its header, now says it cannot be opened safely, instead of showing other files than Extract writes out or losing files when it is changed. Each compressed tar (`.tar.gz`, `.tar.xz`, `.tar.zst`, `.tar.bz2`) and container image is listed anew once, the first time it is opened after the update, as the lists kept of them are made again. | [Archives](files/archives.md#limits) |
