| **Files out of an archive keep their dates, and a link to nothing is no program.** A file copied out of a zip, 7z or tar with **F5**, or extracted with **Ctrl+E**, now keeps the modification date it has in the archive instead of taking the time of copying; a folder made inside a zip or 7z with **F7** is dated now, not 1 January 1980.
