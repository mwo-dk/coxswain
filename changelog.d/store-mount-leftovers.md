| **A disk mounted inside a folder read keeps its text while it is away.** An external drive or an encrypted folder mounted inside your home folder, unplugged or locked, leaves an empty folder: its files' text and vectors now stay, out of Find's results, and nothing is read again when it is back. A folder that is no longer left out but has a part that cannot be read shows its new size at once, and a fault in the helper's indexing shows at once in Settings. | [Removable disks](search/removable-disks.md#a-disk-is-mounted-inside-my-home-folder-what-happens-when-it-is-unplugged) |
