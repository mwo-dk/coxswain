# Sourdough bread

Feed the starter the evening before so it is bubbly in the morning. Mix 500 g strong flour, 350 g water, 100 g starter and 10 g salt, and fold the dough every half hour for two hours. Let it rise in the fridge overnight in a floured basket. Bake in a cast-iron pot at 250 °C, twenty minutes with the lid on and twenty without, until the crust is dark and the loaf sounds hollow.
