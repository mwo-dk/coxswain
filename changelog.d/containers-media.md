| **ffmpeg and ImageMagick: videos, sound and photos the app could not show, and conversions.** Videos and sound the player cannot play (AVI, WMV, MPG, WMA, APE …) show a frame or their waveform, every video gets its length, size and tracks and a thumbnail, songs a waveform, and HEIC, GIMP and RAW photos show in the preview and as thumbnails. *Convert video or audio* makes MP4, MP3 or FLAC copies, the sound alone or a clip, and *Convert or edit pictures* JPEG, PNG or WebP copies, smaller ones, ones without their location, or turned ones, next to the originals, never over them, with progress and Stop, in both apps. An installed ffmpeg is used first, else its container image (about 126 MB); ImageMagick runs in its sealed container first (about 60 MB), else installed under a strict policy.
