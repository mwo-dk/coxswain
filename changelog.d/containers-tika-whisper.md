| **Other formats with Apache Tika.** Turn on *Read other formats with Apache Tika (container)* under Settings → Finding files and Find finds the words in OneNote, XPS, CHM, Outlook .pst and .ost, MHTML, Hangul and Access files, and in old Word, PowerPoint, Publisher and Visio files when LibreOffice is not installed. Off by default; turning it on pulls `docker.io/apache/tika:4.1.0` (about 190 MB) for podman or docker; the server has no network, no folder and no port, and stops after 5 idle minutes. Not on the BSDs or illumos. Read again: only the files of those kinds, once, when you turn it on; nothing else. | [Other formats with Apache Tika](search/tika.md) |
