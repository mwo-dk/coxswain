| **Search looks inside container images, and an image opens at once the second time.** Find knows the files of a `docker save` or `podman save` tar's merged layers by name and by their text, never its blobs (an image over 256 MB once it has been opened). The merged tree and each gzip or zstd layer's places to unpack from are kept in the cache, so a file previewed or copied out comes from near where it is instead of from the start of its layer. New reading: images in searched folders that are new or change are read for search; images search knew before are not read again until they change or the index is rebuilt. | [Container images](files/container-images.md#in-search) | <!-- feature -->
