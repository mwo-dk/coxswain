| **The files of a disk that is not plugged in can still be found and asked about.** Find shows them by name, words and meaning, each marked *on BACKUP1 — not plugged in*, and Ask may use them as sources, marked the same; a line under the list hides them. Opening one says *Plug in BACKUP1 to open this file*, or opens it where the disk is mounted now. Settings says how many of its files can still be found. A folder deleted or renamed on a disk that is still mounted is not taken for one: its files stay out of Find and Ask, as before. Nothing is read or made again for this. | [Removable disks](search/removable-disks.md#what-you-see) | <!-- feature -->
