| **A file out of a large .tar.gz or .tar.zst comes out at once.** The first time a `.tar.gz` is opened, Coxswain keeps a place to unpack it from every 4 MB, and for a `.tar.zst` of several frames where each frame starts; a file taken out later is unpacked from the place before it, not from the start (the last file of a 1.3 GB `.tar.gz`: 0.05 s instead of 2 s). A `.tar.zst` made by `pzstd`, or of several frames, now opens whole: before, only its first frame was read, or none. A `.tar.gz` opened before is listed as it was until it changes. | [Archives as folders](files/archives.md#limits) |
